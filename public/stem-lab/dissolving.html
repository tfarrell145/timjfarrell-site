Where Did It Go?

Word of the day: dissolve

“I can make sugar disappear. Want to see?”

Grab

  • 4 clear glasses of water
  • spoon
  • sugar, salt, sand or dirt, and a little rice or a LEGO

Do

  1. Put a spoon of sugar in a glass and stir. Watch it vanish.
  2. Try salt. Then sand. Then rice.
  3. Line the glasses up. Which ones disappeared and which ones sank?
  4. Taste test the sugar water. Is the sugar really gone?

The big moment

The sugar vanishes. Then they taste the water.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • Is the sugar gone, or hiding?
  • Why didn't the sand disappear?

What's going on (for you)

Sugar and salt dissolve: they break into pieces too tiny to see and spread through the water. They're still there, which is why the water tastes sweet. Sand and rice don't dissolve; they just sink.

If they want more

Warm water vs cold water: which dissolves sugar faster? Leave salt water on a plate in a sunny window for a few days and the salt comes back as crystals.
