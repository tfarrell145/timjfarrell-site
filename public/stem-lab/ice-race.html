Ice Cube Race

Word of the day: melt

“We're having a race. The racers are ice cubes, and the winner is whoever melts first.”

Grab

  • 4 ice cubes
  • 4 small plates
  • salt
  • a sock or mitten
  • a sunny window or warm spot

Do

  1. Give each ice cube a home: one plain, one with salt sprinkled on, one wrapped in a sock, one in their hand (with a towel).
  2. Guess the winner and the loser.
  3. Check every couple of minutes while you do something else.
  4. Bonus: put a string on an ice cube, sprinkle salt, wait a minute, and lift the cube by the string.

The big moment

Lift the ice cube with a string.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • Which one melted first?
  • Did the sock keep it warm or keep it cold?

What's going on (for you)

Ice melts when heat flows into it. A hand is warm, so it gives heat fast. Salt makes ice melt at a colder temperature, which is why roads get salted. The sock is a surprise: it slows heat from getting in, so the wrapped cube lasts longest. Coats work the same way: they hold in the heat you already have.

If they want more

Freeze a toy in a block of ice and let them rescue it with warm water, salt and a spoon.
