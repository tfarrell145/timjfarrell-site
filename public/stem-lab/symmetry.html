Paint Butterflies

Word of the day: symmetry

“I'm going to paint only half a butterfly, and the paper will paint the other half for me.”

Grab

  • paper
  • washable paint
  • a small mirror (optional)

Do

  1. Fold paper in half and open it. Blob paint on one side only.
  2. Fold, press, rub. Open it up. Both sides match.
  3. Hold a mirror on the middle line of a picture or their face. Does it look whole?
  4. Find symmetric things: leaves, faces, cars, letters like A and T.

The big moment

Unfold the paper.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • Are the two sides the same?
  • Which letters look the same in the mirror?

What's going on (for you)

Something is symmetric when one half is a mirror of the other. Lots of living things are close to symmetric, and so are lots of human designs.

If they want more

Draw half a face on folded paper and let them draw the other half.

Look it up

Wikipedia: Reflection symmetry
