STEM Lab

Ten-minute experiments with stuff from the kitchen drawer, in the spirit of Mr. Wizard and Bill Nye.

Events land at 5:30 pm your time, Mon and Thu. Materials are in each event so you can gather ahead.

Lab rules

  1. The kid predicts first, every time. A wrong guess is the best part: that's the surprise.
  2. Their hands do the experiment. You ask the questions and hold the dangerous bits.
  3. Stage one big moment (it's on every card). Pause before it. Let it land.
  4. Use the word of the day out loud, twice, and once at bedtime.
  5. Stop while they still want more. Ten good minutes beats thirty dragged ones.
  6. If the mood's gone, just do the big moment and call it.
