Kit Day

Once a month, something bought, to keep it exciting. Everything else stays household stuff.

Check the age range on the listing before ordering.

2-4KiwiCo Koala Crate monthly subscription
Play-and-craft boxes for ages 2 to 4. Lighter on science than the older crates; good on-ramp.
5-8KiwiCo Kiwi Crate monthly subscription
Build-a-thing science and engineering boxes. The step up at 5, or now with you doing the fiddly parts.
3+Magnetic tiles (Magna-Tiles or similar) one-time
Open-ended building. Pairs with Strong Shapes and the magnet day. Check the tiles are sealed, no loose magnets.
3+Gear building set (Learning Resources Gears! Gears! Gears! or similar) one-time
Turn one gear, the others turn. Big gear vs small gear is the lever trade again, in a circle.
4+Marble run one-time
Ramps, gravity and speed, endlessly rebuildable. Marbles are a choking hazard; supervise, count them back in.
3+Big magnifying glass + bug viewer one-time
Backyard science. Look at salt and sugar crystals after the dissolving day.
3+Kid-safe flashlight + color filters / prism one-time
Follow-up to Shadow Monsters and Rainbow Maker.
