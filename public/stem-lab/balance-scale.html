Hanger Scale

Word of the day: heavier

“Let's build a machine that tells us which thing is heavier, no guessing allowed.”

Grab

  • a coat hanger with notches or a plain hanger
  • two paper or plastic cups
  • string or tape
  • a door handle or cabinet knob to hang it from
  • small things to weigh: blocks, crayons, coins, grapes

Do

  1. Tape or tie a cup to each end of the hanger. Hang it from a door handle. Check it hangs level.
  2. Put a block in one cup. Which side goes down?
  3. How many crayons does it take to balance one block? Count them in one at a time.
  4. Let them pick two mystery things. Guess which is heavier by holding them, then check with the scale.

The big moment

Hang a light thing far out and watch it hold up a heavy thing close in.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • Which side went down? Why?
  • Were your hands right or did the scale surprise you?

What's going on (for you)

A balance compares two weights; the heavier side drops. It's a lever with the pivot in the exact middle, so it links back to The Magic Fork. Counting how many crayons equal one block is a first taste of measuring with units.

If they want more

Make the arms uneven by hanging one cup closer to the middle. Now a lighter thing far out can balance a heavier thing close in, just like the fork.
