Shadow Monsters

Word of the day: shadow

“Let's turn the lights off and make a monster that's bigger than the whole family.”

Grab

  • a flashlight or phone light
  • a blank wall
  • hands, a toy figure, a fork

Do

  1. Turn off the lights. Shine the flashlight at the wall. Put a hand in the way.
  2. Move the hand close to the flashlight. Then close to the wall. Which shadow is bigger?
  3. Make animal shapes: a bird with two hands, a dog with a thumb up.
  4. Shine through a clear glass, a fork, and a sheet of paper. Which make dark shadows?

The big moment

Lights off, hand near the flashlight: the monster fills the wall.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • How do you make the shadow huge?
  • Why can't light go through your hand?

What's going on (for you)

Light travels in straight lines. Anything that blocks it leaves a dark patch behind. Close to the light, your hand blocks a wide cone of light, so the shadow is big; close to the wall it blocks only a little, so the shadow shrinks to hand size.

If they want more

Outside on a sunny morning, trace their shadow with chalk, then check it again at lunch. The sun moved, so the shadow did too.
