Rainbow Maker

Word of the day: rainbow

“White light has a secret. There's a rainbow hiding inside it.”

Grab

  • a clear glass of water
  • a flashlight or a sunny window
  • white paper
  • an old CD if you have one

Do

  1. Put the glass of water at the edge of a table in sunlight (or shine the flashlight through it).
  2. Hold the white paper on the floor where the light lands. Move it until a rainbow shows up.
  3. Tilt a CD under a light and look for colors.
  4. Name the colors in order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple.

The big moment

A rainbow shows up on the floor.

Ask (guess first, then try)

  • Where did the colors come from?
  • Where have you seen a rainbow outside?

What's going on (for you)

White light is all the colors mixed. When light goes into water at an angle it bends, and each color bends a slightly different amount, so they spread apart. Raindrops do this to sunlight to make a rainbow in the sky.

If they want more

With the hose on a sunny day, spray a mist with the sun behind them and they can make their own rainbow.
